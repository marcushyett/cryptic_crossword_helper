How this works

Every clue is answered on its own, one at a time — no grid to fill. The blocks up top are your progress: solved unaided, solved with hints, answer shown.

Hints climb in strength: what the definition is, then the device the setter used, then the raw material it works on, then a letter or two, then the full parsing, and only last the answer itself. Take the lowest rung that unsticks you.

Answers are stored hashed and encoded, so nothing is spoiled by scrolling or by peeking at the page source. Your progress is saved on this device.
